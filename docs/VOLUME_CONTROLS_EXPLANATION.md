# Volume Controls Explanation

## Background Noise System - Volume Controls

### Main Audio Player Volume (Orange Slider)
- **What it controls**: The meditation audio (the voice/guidance)
- **Location**: Main volume slider in the audio player
- **Purpose**: Controls how loud the meditation voice is
- **Range**: 0-100%

### Background Noise Volume (In Drawer)
- **What it controls**: Only the background ambient sound (ocean waves, rain, etc.)
- **Location**: Background noise drawer (click the waves icon 🌊)
- **Purpose**: Controls how loud the background noise is relative to the meditation voice
- **Range**: 0-100%

## How They Work Together

1. **Main Volume**: Controls the overall meditation experience
2. **Background Volume**: Fine-tunes just the ambient background sound
3. **Result**: You can have meditation voice at 80% and ocean waves at 30% for perfect balance

## Example Settings

- **Meditation Voice**: 80% (main slider)
- **Ocean Waves**: 30% (background drawer)
- **Result**: Clear meditation guidance with subtle ocean ambiance

## Why This Design?

- **Simplicity**: Only adjust background noise when needed
- **Control**: Fine-tune the balance between voice and ambiance
- **Intuitive**: Main volume for overall experience, background for ambiance
